Before you book

A few useful answers.

Clear expectations make getting help simpler.

Can you help if I don't know what's wrong?

Yes. Describe what happens and include the computer model if you know it. A desktop fault assessment is £20 for up to 30 minutes, credited towards an accepted repair. The charge and scope are agreed beforehand.

Are replacement parts included?

No. The £49 desktop fitting price is labour for a straightforward compatible RAM or SSD installation. Compatibility is checked before you buy. Components, software licences, cloning and moving data are separate.

Do I need to back up my files?

Yes, before a reinstall, formatting or storage changes. Important files need a verified backup and the specific operation must be agreed. If no backup exists, discuss it first. A suspected failing drive needs specialist assessment.

Can I drop off my computer?

Appointment arrangements and location are confirmed by email. Do not bring a device to an address without an agreed appointment. Home visits and remote sessions are available for suitable jobs.

What happens during remote support?

You contact me first, approve any screen sharing or control, and remain present. Never send passwords, banking details or private files in your enquiry. Remote help is limited to suitable software issues.

Do you handle every type of repair?

No. Board-level repairs, liquid damage, specialist data recovery, business servers and urgent business-critical support need a specialist. If the problem is outside my scope, I'll explain that before accepting the job.

Is a repair guaranteed?

No outcome can be guaranteed before diagnosis. Assessments and time-based sessions cover the agreed professional time and checks. Further repairs require your approval. Your statutory consumer rights remain unchanged.
