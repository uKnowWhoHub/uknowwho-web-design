Questions, answered

Before you ask.

Still unsure about something? Send a message and I'll give you a straightforward answer.

01 How long does a website take? +

A straightforward business website normally takes 7–14 days after I receive your text, images and feedback. More complex projects are scheduled separately.

02 Do I own the website and domain? +

Yes. Your domain and website account stay in your name. I can help set everything up, but you remain in control.

03 Can I change things after launch? +

Yes. You can make changes yourself, request one-off updates, or choose the monthly care plan for ongoing help.

04 Will it work properly on phones? +

Absolutely. Every website is designed and checked for phones, tablets and desktop screens before launch.

05 Can you guarantee first place on Google? +

No honest designer can guarantee that. I build clean, search-friendly pages and can help you connect your Google Business Profile, but rankings depend on competition, reputation and ongoing marketing.
